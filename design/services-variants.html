Prototype · not part of the site

From the first sketch to launch.

Four ways to present the six service categories. Same content and tokens as the site (dark, sharp cards, round buttons); only the layout and interaction differ.

A

Index

A big typographic list. Hover a row: the others dim, the row opens with the lead and a picture. Fast to scan, very editorial. Closest to the current services section on the home page.

B

Bento

One mosaic of cards of different sizes: you see all six at once, and the size hints at what matters most (Design is the biggest). Hover inverts a card to white.

C

Stack

Scroll tells the story in order: each category is a full card that slides over the previous one. Best for “from the first sketch to launch” as a process.

D

Split

Names on the left, a sticky detail panel on the right that changes as you hover or tap a name. Calm, nothing jumps, lots of room for the detail.
