Prototype · not part of the site

Services, not as a list.

Four unusual ways to show the six categories — inspired by Metalab's calm split and The Gradient's big typographic statements, but kept in our dark, sharp, round-chip language. Hover and scroll each one.

A

Reel rows

Giant category names. On hover the row floods white from the bottom, the name slides out and a marquee of its services runs through the row. Zero chrome, all typography.

B

Curtains

Pinned, full-screen. Each category is a curtain that wipes up over the previous one; the colour alternates black / grey / white. The huge word slides in slightly slower than the curtain, for depth.

C

Cursor gallery

A calm list until you move the mouse. A picture follows the cursor with a little inertia and tilt, the hovered row nudges right and shows its services, the rest fade out.

D

Rolling word

One sentence — “We do …” — and the last word rolls like a slot-machine reel as you scroll. Below it the description, services and a progress bar change in step.
